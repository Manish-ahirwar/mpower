About M POWER

Precision Fastening. Built for Construction.

M POWER is the fastening and fixing brand of RAHJ Transworld General Traders FZC, operating from CWS-IV-225549, Amber Gem Tower, Ajman Freezone, UAE. The brand supplies construction-grade fasteners to contractors and fabricators across the GCC.

The range covers self-drilling screws, blind rivets, threaded rods and nuts, shear studs, saddle washers, butyl and purlin tapes, screw colour caps and filler blocks, M POWER branded silicone sealant, and a full line of cold room accessories, including door hardware, PVC ribbed curtains and railing.

M POWER products serve building and construction, civil engineering, MEP, steel fabrication and cold storage projects. Self-drilling screws are available with Ruspert surface treatment for coastal and high-humidity sites where standard zinc plating corrodes early.

Stocked lines dispatch within 3 working days from Ajman Freezone. Technical queries (size selection, coating choice, drilling capacity) are handled directly over WhatsApp by the product team.

Surface Technology

Triple-Layer Corrosion Protection

Ruspert is a three-layer coating system: a metal zinc base, an inorganic high corrosion resistant layer, and a ceramic top coat. Together they far outlast plain zinc plating across humid, coastal and cold-room conditions.

Standard option · specify at order
Up to 2,000 hrs Neutral salt spray to ISO 9227 with no red rust. Standard electro-zinc shows red rust in just 72 to 120 hours.
  • Three fastener types. Applied across every M POWER self-drilling screw: Bi-Metal SS304, Carbon Steel C1022A and SS410.
  • Gulf-ready. Engineered for Middle East humidity, coastal salt air and cold-room condensation.
  • Chemical resistance. Also withstands acid rain at 5% acetic acid over 30 cycles.
